Trust & Legal

By using AppContributor, founders and contributors agree that payouts depend on review outcomes, contest handling, and verified payment logs. Approved submissions can receive full or partial payout.

  • One submission per contributor per task: resubmitting the same task is not allowed.
  • Contributors must submit original reasoning and code that matches the issue.
  • Admins must give clear outcomes (approved, partial, rejected) with review notes.
  • Contests are for fairness and policy checks, not unlimited retries.
  • All payout actions are timestamped and linked to PayPal/Visa method records.
  • Rejected submissions must include reason + review note for transparency.
  • Contests are reviewed with preserved submission and payout audit history.
  • Webhook/provider confirmations serve as the source of truth for payment status.
